FOLD THE TOP CORNERS IN
Take the top right corner and bring it down so its edge lines up with the middle crease. Press it flat with your thumb. Do the same on the left.
WHY
These two folds put the weight at the nose, which is what keeps the plane pointed forward.
FOLD TERMS
Valley fold
The crease dips down, like a valley. The paper folds toward you.
Center line
The crease down the middle you made in step 01.

# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Flight School welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Accept correction and take responsibility when harm is caused.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, sexualized language, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider harmful or inappropriate.

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's repository-owner contact tools.
Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible.
